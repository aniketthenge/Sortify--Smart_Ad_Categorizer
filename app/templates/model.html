How it works

  1. Collect — Playwright opens Lokmat Times section pages in headless Chromium, scrolls to trigger lazy ad widgets, and extracts Taboola sponsored cards and Google ad frames. Editorial recommendations are discarded.
  2. Represent — headline + description + advertiser + domain words are turned into TF-IDF word n-grams (1–2) and character n-grams (2–5). Character n-grams handle Devanagari and brand names like elitematrimony.
  3. Predict — a class-balanced Logistic Regression outputs a probability per category.
  4. Blend rules — a curated keyword lexicon per category contributes 25% of the score when it fires. This steadies the model on short, unseen ads and explains the decision.
  5. Review loop — predictions under 35% confidence are flagged. Your corrections on the Ads page are stored and included the next time you retrain.
